Controller and scope

This notice covers SportiPlan for Android and iOS, its product pages and support. The publisher and controller for the support and website processing described here is Ali Babi (BabiLabs)

Your organisation’s records

SportiPlan is a local management tool for clubs, trainers, studios and sports schools. Depending on use, it stores organisation and trainer details; member and guardian names, birth dates, contact details, addresses, identity numbers, notes and member photos; groups, schedules, attendance, memberships, contributions, payment and expense records; and app preferences. BabiLabs does not receive these records through normal app operation and does not operate a cloud database for them. The organisation using the app normally determines the purposes and legal basis for its records and is responsible for its own privacy information. Direct requests about club records to that organisation.

Local devices, permissions and messages

Connect can transfer selected records directly between devices you pair on a local network. Messages are authenticated and encrypted; no BabiLabs relay server is used. Pair only authorised devices and remove access when no longer needed. Camera access supports pairing QR codes and member photos; photo access supports images you select. Permissions can be changed in device settings. Local reminders are handled on the device. If you open a prepared WhatsApp message, its recipient number and message text are passed to WhatsApp or its web service; review the recipient and content before continuing. WhatsApp privacy information.

Google ML Kit on Android

The Android app uses Google ML Kit Barcode Scanning 17.3.0 through mobile_scanner for QR scanning. According to Google’s ML Kit data disclosure, the SDK collects device and app information, per-installation identifiers, performance metrics and scanner usage events for diagnostics and usage analytics. Google receives these technical data over HTTPS, which encrypts them in transit. This SDK communication means the app can transmit technical data to external servers. It does not automatically give BabiLabs access to locally stored member records. Technical SDK data and your organisation’s local records are distinct.

Backups and security

App files use the platform’s private app storage; this is not a promise that every local file is separately encrypted. Password-protected SportiPlan backups are encrypted before export. Backups without a password do not provide confidential password protection. Keep files and passwords secure. BabiLabs cannot recover your backup password. System backups and device migration may also copy app data according to your Android/iOS settings and provider. A destination you select for a backup or shared file processes it under its own rules.

Store purchases

Optional feature tiers are one-time in-app purchases through Google Play or the Apple App Store, rather than SportiPlan subscriptions. The store handles checkout and payment details. SportiPlan processes product, transaction/verification and entitlement information to unlock and restore purchases. BabiLabs does not receive your card details through the app. These app purchases are separate from membership payments you record for your organisation. Store operation, refunds and privacy are also governed by the relevant store rules. Apple privacy · Google privacy.

Support and website data

Support data is collected electronically through your email, and website connection data automatically when pages are requested. If you email babilabs@duck.com, we receive your address, message, attachments you choose to send and technical details you provide. We use them to respond, troubleshoot and handle related claims. Do not send member lists, identity documents, children’s data, backup files or passwords. We use email and forwarding service providers, including DuckDuckGo Email Protection for the contact address and GMX (1&1 Mail & Media GmbH, Germany) for the receiving mailbox. GMX privacy Email Protection privacy. These static pages use GitHub Pages. GitHub processes connection data such as IP address, request time and browser/request information to deliver and secure the website. GitHub privacy information. On these static web pages, we add no analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, contact forms or non-essential cookies. This website statement does not exclude the Android SDK processing described above. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

Purposes and legal grounds

For processing by BabiLabs, where GDPR applies, requested contractual support is based on Article 6(1)(b); general enquiries, service security and establishing or defending claims on Article 6(1)(f), subject to the balancing of your rights; and applicable legal duties on Article 6(1)(c). Relevant Turkish grounds include Law No. 6698 Article 5(2)(c), (ç), (e) and (f), as applicable to the specific purpose. Permission in the operating system does not replace a legal basis for entering other people’s data. We do not make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about you solely by automated processing.

Recipients and international processing

Recipients may include email/forwarding and hosting providers for their respective functions, and authorities or professional advisers when legally required or necessary to handle claims. Google receives the technical ML Kit data described above. App stores and a communication or storage provider you use process data for their respective services. BabiLabs operates in Türkiye; contacting support from another country makes your message available there. Providers may process data in other countries. Local club records do not move to Türkiye merely because the developer is based there. Where applicable, international transfers require an appropriate mechanism under GDPR Chapter V and/or KVKK Article 9; ask us for information about the safeguards relevant to your request.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until managed or deleted on the devices holding them. Uninstalling may remove private app storage, but does not erase exported files, other paired devices or system backups. Retention of club and accounting records is the organisation’s responsibility. Support messages are retained while needed to resolve the enquiry and any related follow-up or claims, then deleted unless a legal retention obligation applies. Relevant criteria are whether the matter is closed, whether a dispute remains and the applicable statutory periods. Hosting and store providers determine retention for their own processing. Request deletion of support correspondence using the contact above.

Subject to applicable conditions, GDPR rights include access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability, objection to processing based on legitimate interests and withdrawal of consent where consent is the basis. You can complain to the relevant supervisory authority, including in your EU country of residence, work or alleged infringement. EU supervisory authorities. Under KVKK Article 11, rights include learning whether data is processed, information about processing and purposes, recipients, correction, deletion/destruction where conditions are met, notification of relevant actions to recipients, objection to adverse results arising exclusively from automated analysis and compensation for unlawful damage. Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority. We may request proportionate information to verify identity; do not send an identity document unsolicited.

Minors and sensitive records

The app is intended for people administering organisations, not as an online service for children to submit their own data. Organisations remain responsible for lawful processing of minors’ and guardians’ records. Do not enter health information, identity numbers or other sensitive details unless necessary and supported by an appropriate legal basis and safeguards. This notice does not supply consent on behalf of members.

Updates

We update this notice when relevant features, processing or requirements change. The date on this page identifies the current text; a notice update does not by itself authorise a new incompatible use of data.
