Your club. Your plan.

Local management for sports clubs, trainers, studios and sports schools. Organise members, training, attendance and membership payments on Android and iOS.

SportiPlan stores your organisation’s records locally and supports direct exchange between paired devices. Optional feature tiers are purchased through Google Play or the Apple App Store. Learn how the app works, how data is handled and how to contact support below.
