01 / One tap. One orbit. One more try.

Orbit Hop '89 is an offline pixel arcade game about timing your escape from one planet's orbit to the next. Chase a new personal best or travel through a galaxy of challenges. No account, ads or in-app purchases are required.

  1. Watch the orbit. Your ship circles the current planet automatically. Look for your next target and a clear route.
  2. Tap to launch. A tap sends the ship along its current direction of travel, tangent to the orbit. Time the launch so the path meets another planet.
  3. Land and repeat. The next planet captures the ship into orbit. Watch for changing orbit directions, hazards and countdowns before your next hop.

Flight tip / The ship leaves along the orbit's tangent, not straight out from the planet's centre. Wait for alignment, then tap.

02 / Choose your mission

SURVIVE & SCORE

Arcade Endless

Keep hopping, survive changing waves and chase points, hops and survival-time records. There is no fixed overall time limit. Each orbit has a limited stay, and a miss or fatal hazard ends the run. Watch the countdown and keep moving.

EXPLORE & PROGRESS

Galaxy Journey

Complete levels, earn stars and unlock the next challenge. Explore Blue Nebula, Asteroid Belt, UFO Invasion, Solar Storm and Dark Matter. Journey uses local checkpoints to let you retry sections; its progress is separate from Arcade scores.

03 / Records worth another run

Highscores

The Arcade leaderboard keeps a local top ten with three-character names. Personal best score, survival time and hop count are also saved locally. Scores are not uploaded or shared across devices. Journey records track stars, best scores and completion times separately.

Checkpoints

In Galaxy Journey, use the available Continue / CP option to return to a saved checkpoint. A retry restores the checkpoint's banked score; points from the failed section are lost. Restarting from a checkpoint affects the stars available for that attempt. Replaying a level does not erase your previously earned best records.

04 / Flight desk FAQ

Can I play without an internet connection?

Yes. Gameplay, levels and audio are bundled with the app. You need a connection to visit these pages, send email or use app-store services.

Why does my ship miss the next planet?

A tap launches tangentially from your current orbit. Try a slightly earlier or later tap and pay attention to orbit direction. Avoid tapping repeatedly during a flight.

Why did my Arcade run end while I was orbiting?

Arcade orbits have a limited stay. Normal orbits allow ten seconds; special planets or attacks can shorten that window. Launch before the displayed countdown expires and watch for hazard warnings.

Why is the next Journey level locked?

Complete the preceding level to unlock the next one. Later worlds unlock through progress in the previous world. Progress is saved on the current device.

How do I turn off music or sound?

Open the game's Settings and switch Music or Sound off. The choices are saved separately. If audio is missing, also check your device volume and silent-mode settings.

Can I move scores and saves to another device?

The game has no account-based cloud sync or manual save-transfer service. Device backups or migration may behave differently by platform; do not rely on them as a guaranteed transfer. See the backup information.

How do I delete scores or all my progress?

Settings → Clear High Scores removes leaderboard entries after confirmation, but keeps personal records and Journey progress. For a full local reset, clear the app's storage on Android or use Delete App on iOS. This loses local progress; manage backups separately. Read the deletion instructions first.

My save is missing, or I see a save error. What should I do?

If the game offers a retry, try saving again. Check that your device has free storage. Avoid clearing app data or uninstalling while investigating, as that can remove progress. Email support with the build label and steps leading to the problem. BabiLabs has no server copy from which to restore local records.

How do I report a bug?

Include your device model, OS version, the app version/build label shown in the game, game mode and level, what happened and how to reproduce it. A screenshot is helpful if it contains no private information. No diagnostic upload happens automatically through this page.
